## Usage boundary

- Treat the original supplied logo as locked artwork.
- Allowed: lossless crop, transparent background, responsive sizing, optimized SVG/PNG delivery, accessible text alternative, and a symbol-only crop only if the owner explicitly approves that use.
- Not allowed without owner approval: redraw, restyle, recolor, change typography, change slogan, alter spacing/proportions, add effects, or generate a visually different replacement.
